## Important interpretation notes

1. All four URLs are indexed, fetch successfully, allow indexing, and have matching Google-selected canonicals. There is no technical-indexing repair required before content work.
2. The three jail pages use the same shared page structure. Their next improvements must add facility-specific usefulness without copying the same new paragraphs across every jail page.
3. The 77 detected inbound links are largely generated by sitewide/template navigation. The optimization work should add a smaller number of locally relevant contextual links from county, city, and related facility pages.
4. West Palm Beach is materially thinner than the jail pages and targets a commercial local intent rather than facility-navigation intent. It requires a different content plan.
5. Positions are impression-weighted averages, not a single fixed ranking.
