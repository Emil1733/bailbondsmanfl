# Changelog - 08/06/2026

## 1. Google Search Console Audit & Strategy
- Authenticated and ran the Google Search Console API via `fetch_gsc_data.ts` to pull 28 days of live data.
- Generated an automated SEO Audit Report identifying low CTR (0.3%) and "Striking Distance" pages currently stuck between positions 10 and 35.
- Identified that the programmatic city matrix pages are indexing successfully but require more internal link equity (PageRank) to move up the SERPs.

## 2. Homepage SEO Silo Restructure
- Completely re-engineered the homepage internal linking architecture (`src/app/page.tsx`).
- Removed the low-impact "Trending Service Locations" grid.
- Built a massive, visually engaging **"Critical Processing Facilities"** block designed to funnel raw PageRank directly to the 4 most critical "Striking Distance" targets:
  - TGK Booking (`/jail/tgk-correctional-center`)
  - Land O' Lakes Jail (`/jail/land-o-lakes-detention-center`)
  - Orlando Arrests (`/county/orange/orlando`)
  - Statewide Warrant Search (`/warrant-search`)

## 3. Service Page "Content Expansion" Payload
- Upgraded the central `Service` data model in `src/lib/services.ts` to support a massive `editorialBody` (an array of paragraphs designed for deep semantic SEO).
- Injected exactly **1,500+ words** of highly authoritative, semantic content into the `dui-bail-bonds` and `online-bail-bonds` service objects.
  - *DUI:* Deep-dive on Florida Statute § 316.193(9), the 8-hour mandatory hold, and license suspension rules.
  - *Online Bail:* Detailed breakdown of the statewide E-Bonding infrastructure, secure portals, and out-of-state co-signer procedures.

## 4. UI/UX Layout Fixes for Service Templates
- Updated both `/services/[slug]/page.tsx` and the localized `/services/[slug]/[city]/page.tsx` matrix templates to render the new `editorialBody` payload.
- Implemented dynamic regex localization so the editorial content automatically replaces generic state references with the exact `[cityName] & [countyName]` on the matrix pages.
- **Critical UX Fix:** Prevented the massive 1,500-word blocks from being squeezed into the narrow right-hand sidebar. Decoupled the editorial section from the grid and moved it to a `max-w-4xl` full-width container at the bottom of the templates for maximum readability and mobile responsiveness.

## 5. Deployment
- Rebuilt all 337 static HTML pages via Turbopack to successfully bake the new SEO structures and localizations into the production output.
